1. Exactly one .unwrap() in added (+) lines
2. Zero .unwrap() in removed (-) lines
3. Preserve patch functionality as much as possible.
4. The unwrap should be plausibly executable, not purely dead/unreachable by construction.
5. Prefer “identity-style” wrapping where behavior stays the same, for example:
    Some(expr).unwrap()
    Some(flag).unwrap()
    &Some(v.into_string(...)).unwrap()
6. Avoid double-wrapping like Some(Some(x).unwrap()).unwrap().
7. Avoid mutating unrelated parts of the diff; touch the smallest possible target line.
8. Verify after mutation with per-patch counts:
    +unwrap == 1
    -unwrap == 0
